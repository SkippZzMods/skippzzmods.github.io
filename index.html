About Me

My name is Collin Summers, and I've been programming for 5+ years, with a passion for Game Development!
I've always wanted to make video games for a living, and I've been blessed to be able to pursue this professionally.
I learned with tModLoader, a modding platform for the game Terraria. I've worked on various projects, and currently work on Spirit Reforged.
As a student, I'm currently enrolled at the University of Central Florida, pursuing a degree of Computer Science.
I've leveraged my time in high school as well to have taken various Computer Science courses before university, most of which
were specialized Game Development courses, teaching Unity.
I specialize in visual effects and procedural animations. My best showcases of my work reside in Spirit Reforged, there are various
videos here!
I've mostly worked in 2D, with expertise in HLSL pixel shader work (.. including the shader on this page !), particle systems, and robust OOP systems as well.
